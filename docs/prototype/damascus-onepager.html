03 · DRAFT COPY

Story

ESC ✕

I grew up in Damascus, a city where craftsmen build patterns like the one behind this page out of thousands of small pieces, each one cut to fit.

I studied accounting and worked as an accountant and an English teacher before I wrote my first line of code. In 2021 I started teaching myself to program: JavaScript and TypeScript first, then Vue, Nuxt and React, and enough Node to build my own APIs.

Today I work at Scala, a digital-signage company. I started in technical support, and I now also work as a frontend developer in the services team, where my work on the Quintet platform has shipped to production.

Accounting taught me precision, teaching taught me to explain, and support taught me what users actually run into. I bring all three to the frontend.
